# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People exploring an interactive web experience, including bubble-tea fans and design or development audiences interested in scroll-driven storytelling. They visit to enjoy a short, self-guided visual journey rather than complete a transaction or read a detailed tutorial.

## Product Purpose

Turn the process of making bubble tea into an immersive interactive showcase. Scrolling should feel like physically advancing the recipe from raw tea to the existing finished-drink carousel. Success means visitors understand the sequence without lengthy explanation, enjoy reversing and replaying it, and reach the existing flavor interactions without a perceptible break.

## Brand Personality

Bold, playful, kinetic. The experience uses direct language, saturated color, crisp vector forms, and purposeful movement. It should feel like a confident graphic animation brought under the visitor's control.

## Anti-references

- Photorealistic luxury café advertising or subdued lifestyle photography.
- Beige editorial layouts, delicate serif styling, or quiet magazine aesthetics.
- Dense educational infographics that explain every action with paragraphs and diagrams.
- Decorative glassmorphism, generic card grids, or repeated fade-and-rise section reveals.
- Motion that plays independently of scroll, traps the user, or cannot be reversed cleanly.

## Design Principles

- Make scrolling perform the recipe rather than merely reveal it.
- Preserve physical continuity: ingredients and tools move into the next step instead of disappearing and being recreated.
- Spend motion on transitions that explain cause and effect.
- Keep the current finished cup and flavor carousel as the payoff, not a separate page.
- Make the complete experience equally intentional on desktop and modern mobile devices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for readable text, controls, focus states, and color contrast. Preserve native scrolling and keyboard access. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` with a non-pinned, low-motion presentation of the same eight steps. Keep process content meaningful without animation and avoid continuous screen-reader announcements while scrolling.
